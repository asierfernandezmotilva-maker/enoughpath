FIRE by age

Health insurance before Medicare

Medicare starts at 65, so anyone who retires earlier needs another way to pay for health coverage. The main options are COBRA from your last employer, a Marketplace plan with premium tax credits based on income, a spouse's plan or a job that includes insurance.

How income affects Marketplace costs

Premium tax credits are based on your expected household income for the year. In early retirement, income is what you choose to realize: withdrawals from traditional accounts, Roth conversions and capital gains all count. Spending from a taxable account or Roth contributions can keep reported income lower. Check the current rules and amounts on HealthCare.gov for the year you apply.

Budgeting for it

Put the premium and expected out-of-pocket costs into your yearly spending before you work out your number. Every $10,000 a year of health costs adds $303,030 to the portfolio needed at Morningstar's 40-year rate of 3.3%, but only until 65; a bridge fund for those years is an alternative to raising the whole target.

Medicare at 65

The Initial Enrollment Period lasts seven months: the three months before the month you turn 65, that month and the three after.

Questions people ask

How much is health insurance before Medicare?

It depends on the option and your income: COBRA can cost up to 102% of the full plan premium, while Marketplace costs depend on the plan and on the tax credit your income qualifies for. Price your own case on HealthCare.gov.

How to get health insurance before Medicare kicks in?

Losing job-based coverage can qualify you for a Special Enrollment Period on the Marketplace, outside open enrollment; COBRA is the other route from your last employer. A spouse's employer plan is another route.

Do I need health insurance if I have Medicare?

Many people add a Medicare supplement or a Part D drug plan to Original Medicare, or choose a Medicare Advantage plan. Medicare.gov compares the options.

Related

Retiring at 62 and retiring at 55 include health insurance in the plan.
