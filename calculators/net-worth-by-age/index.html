How your percentile is worked out

The calculator finds the age group of the head of your family and places your net worth between the two nearest of that group's 99 percentiles, interpolating in a straight line. Below the 1st or above the 99th percentile it says so instead of guessing. Everything runs in your browser.

What counts as net worth

In the survey, net worth is the value of what a family owns (homes, vehicles, businesses, retirement and other accounts) minus what it owes (mortgages, student and car loans, credit cards). A FIRE portfolio is narrower: it counts only what you can invest and draw from, so use the FIRE calculator for that.

These are statistics about US families, not targets. Your own goal depends on what you spend, as the FIRE calculator shows. See the disclaimer.

Questions people ask

What percentile is $1,000,000 net worth?

Among all US families, $1,000,000 is at about the 78th percentile. For families headed by someone 45 to 54 it is about the 76th, and for 35 to 44 about the 87th.

What is a good net worth at my age?

The survey shows what is typical rather than what is good. For families headed by someone 35 to 44, the median is $156,000 and the middle half sit between $17,992 and $466,962. A goal of your own depends on what you spend, which is what the FIRE calculator works out.
